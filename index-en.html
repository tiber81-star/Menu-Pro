Why a chef built a simple tool — when every other software got complicated.

My name is Sébastien. I'm a chef.

Like you, I've spent whole evenings writing my menus by hand. Trying to balance the courses, avoiding repeats, rethinking the same things every single week. And the next day, in the middle of the rush, there was always that moment when I forgot a recipe. I searched through my files, I lost precious minutes.

So I tried the existing software. All the same: too heavy, too expensive, too many features nobody ever uses. Stock management, accounting, staff scheduling… That's not what I was looking for. And worst of all: you had to create an account, log in, be online. Just to write a menu.

"I wasn't looking for management software. I was looking for a tool that does one thing: give me a menu in one click, at home, without depending on anyone. And when I forget a recipe mid-service, gives it to me in a second."

So I built MenuPro. And today, I can't live without it. Here's why.

What used to take me an hour now takes me two minutes.

You know that moment when, mid-rush, you forget the recipe of a dish? It used to be pure panic. Now, every dish on my menu is a direct link to its recipe on Google. I click, the recipe opens, I'm saved.

The result of MenuPro isn't a toy. It's a print-ready document — A4, A5 or A3. I display it in the kitchen, in the dining room. It becomes the base of my organisation: orders, prep, service.

No signup. No password. No internet. Your data stays on your computer, and nobody else has access to it. The program works even if your router dies.

📝 My dishes, my recipes, my rules

💾 Save and export in one click
