The story behind MenuPro

Why a chef built this software — and why it changes everything.

My name is Sébastien, I'm a chef. I started in a brasserie: one menu, one daily special. Easy, little planning needed.

Then I changed the type of restaurant I worked in. I moved to a worker canteen kitchen. Everything changed: I had to create 4 different daily specials every single day.

And there's a constraint nobody understands until they've lived it: we get delivered every Monday late morning, and my order is sent on Friday.

Which means: on Friday, I already had to know my menus all the way to the following Monday. Before MenuPro, I was writing my menus for 15 days at the end of each week. Hours lost, every single week.

"Before MenuPro, I spent hours every Friday writing 15 days of menus. Now I generate the base in 30 seconds, then adapt based on how busy the restaurant is."

This program helped me save an enormous amount of time. I have my base of menus, and I adjust based on the restaurant's foot traffic.

And since the menus are printable, they can just as easily be displayed in a school canteen or a care home. The pork / chicken / fish pictograms let you identify every dish at a glance.

— Sébastien, chef and creator of MenuPro
